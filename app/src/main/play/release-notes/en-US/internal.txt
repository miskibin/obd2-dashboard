The car screen is rebuilt around what your dashboard does not tell you. Speed and revs are gone - your car has those - and in their place are six live tiles: instant consumption, oil, coolant, intake air, battery and long-term fuel trim. Each draws its own last half minute and the threshold you set on the phone.

Two more tabs: stored fault codes with what each one means, readiness monitors and the ECU's freeze frame; and session controls - start a recording, read a freeze frame, clear codes or check your thresholds, the last two only while parked.
